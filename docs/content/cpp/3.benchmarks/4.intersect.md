Benchmarks for geometric intersection computations. Reported times are mean wall-clock measurements over 10 runs. Test meshes are variants of the [Stanford Dragon](https://github.com/xlabmedical/trueform/tree/main/benchmarks/data) remeshed to 58K–1M polygons.

## Isocontours

Extraction of isocontours from a scalar field defined on vertices. The scalar field is Euclidean distance from the mesh centroid; isovalues are evenly spaced across the field range. TrueForm uses `tf::make_isocontours`; [VTK](https://vtk.org/) uses `vtkContourFilter`; [libigl](https://libigl.github.io/) uses `igl::isolines`. Results shown for 16 isovalues.

Source: [TrueForm](https://github.com/xlabmedical/trueform/blob/main/benchmarks/intersect/isocontours-tf-impl.cpp), [VTK](https://github.com/xlabmedical/trueform/blob/main/benchmarks/intersect/isocontours-vtk-impl.cpp), [libigl](https://github.com/xlabmedical/trueform/blob/main/benchmarks/intersect/isocontours-igl-impl.cpp)

## Mesh–Mesh Curves

Intersection curves between two overlapping meshes. The second mesh is a translated copy, offset by 50% of the bounding box extent along its longest axis. TrueForm uses `tf::make_intersection_curves`; [CGAL](https://www.cgal.org/) uses `CGAL::Polygon_mesh_processing::surface_intersection`.

Source: [TrueForm](https://github.com/xlabmedical/trueform/blob/main/benchmarks/intersect/mesh_mesh_curves-tf-impl.cpp), [CGAL](https://github.com/xlabmedical/trueform/blob/main/benchmarks/intersect/mesh_mesh_curves-cgal-impl.cpp)
